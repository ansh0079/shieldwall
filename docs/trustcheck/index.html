Multi-Pillar Shopping Defense

Most blockers only check a static domain list. TrustCheck runs real-time forensic tests on every store you visit.

🛡️

Brand Typosquat Detection

Scammers register domains like amaz0n.com or nike-clearance-outlet.com. TrustCheck de-leets characters and checks token boundaries to catch impostors instantly.

⏱️

Domain Age vs. Archive Timeline

Buying an expired 10-year-old domain is a favorite scam tactic. TrustCheck cross-references registry creation with Wayback Machine history to detect newly repurposed sites.

💳

Payment Hijacking & Skimmers

Detects checkout forms submitting card details to unapproved third-party servers before you click Pay (Magecart & form-hijack protection).

📉

Search Price Outliers

When searching on Google Shopping, TrustCheck compares each seller's price against the results median. 70%-off liquidation lures are flagged before you leave search.

🏛️

Corporate Registry Verification

Extracts claimed company registration and EU/UK VAT numbers from footers, validating whether the registered entity is active, brand-new, or dissolved.

👥

Verified Buyer Signal Network

Separates real order confirmations from fake 5-star claims. Reports from actual checkout confirmations build transparent, crowd-sourced trust metrics.

Your browsing history belongs to you.

TrustCheck never transmits complete page URLs, form data, or search terms. Only the registrable domain name is checked against safety registries. No account required, zero tracking cookies, and completely open source.
