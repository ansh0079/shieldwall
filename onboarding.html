Welcome to QuietBrowse

Your privacy shield is active. Here's what QuietBrowse does for you.

🚫

Blocks ads & trackers

Full EasyList coverage stops ads, trackers, and annoyances. The toolbar badge shows how many requests were blocked on each page.

🧠

Learns new trackers

Like Privacy Badger, QuietBrowse watches third-party domains. If something follows you across sites, it gets auto-blocked.

🔒

Password shield

Copying from a password field puts random junk on your clipboard instead. Scripts that secretly reveal passwords get re-masked.

⚠️

Phishing warnings

Lookalike domains, risky addresses, and unencrypted login pages trigger warnings. The popup shows a trust score for each site.

✂️

Element zapper

Right-click any page and choose Zap element on this page, or use the button in the popup, to permanently hide annoyances.

📊

Settings & stats

Open Settings from the popup to manage your whitelist, learned trackers, zapped elements, and view blocking stats over time.

Quick tips

  • Click the big shield in the popup to turn protection on or off globally.
  • If a site breaks, click Fix this site in the popup, then reload.
  • Pin QuietBrowse from the puzzle-piece menu for quick access.
  • No data leaves your device — see our Privacy Policy.
